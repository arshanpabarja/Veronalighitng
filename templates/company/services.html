Our Process

1

Discovery & Consultation

We meet with you to understand your vision, requirements, budget, and timeline. Our team conducts site assessments and gathers technical information needed for accurate design.

Duration: 1–2 weeks

2

Design & Planning

Our lighting designers create custom concepts with 3D renderings, technical specifications, and detailed lighting plans. We incorporate your feedback and refine the design until it's perfect.

Duration: 2–4 weeks

3

Detailed Specification & Procurement

We finalize specifications, source all materials, and provide detailed documentation. You'll have complete transparency on fixtures, controls, and electrical requirements.

Duration: 1–2 weeks

4

Installation & Commissioning

Our certified installers handle all electrical work, fixture mounting, and system integration. We conduct thorough testing and commission all controls before handoff.

Duration: Varies by project scope

5

Training & Ongoing Support

We train your team on operating the lighting system and controls. Then we provide ongoing support, maintenance, and service to ensure optimal performance.

Ongoing: Maintenance plans available

Industry Specializations

Retail Spaces

Precision lighting that highlights products and creates immersive shopping environments. We design systems that enhance merchandise visibility while setting the perfect ambiance.

Expertise: Track lighting, accent lighting, color-accurate spotlights, smart dimming.

Hospitality

Dynamic lighting solutions for hotels, restaurants, and lounges. We create mood lighting that adapts from morning through evening service, enhancing guest experience.

Expertise: Ambiance lighting, scene programming, guest-facing controls, energy efficiency.

Office & Workplace

Human-centric lighting designed to improve productivity, comfort, and well-being. Our solutions support circadian rhythms while reducing eye strain and energy consumption.

Expertise: Task lighting, ambient systems, circadian controls, flicker-free tech.

Residential

Custom lighting for homes that combines beauty with functionality. From living spaces to kitchens, we design systems that adapt to your lifestyle.

Expertise: Dimming, color selection, architectural integration, smart home compatibility.

Facade & Exterior

Bold architectural lighting that transforms buildings and landscapes. We design facade systems that showcase structure while creating stunning nighttime presence.

Expertise: RGB systems, facade accents, landscape lighting, DMX control, weatherproofing.

Museums & Galleries

Specialized lighting for art and artifacts. CRI 95+ spotlights ensure true color rendering while UV-filtered systems protect valuable pieces.

Expertise: High CRI, UV filtration, artwork accentuation, conservation-grade systems.
