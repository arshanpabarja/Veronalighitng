How It Started

We started with a clear idea: lighting should be reliable, energy-efficient, and beautiful. In the early days, we focused on learning every detail—optics, materials, heat management, and the craft that makes fixtures last.

As our reputation grew, architects, contractors, and homeowners trusted us with larger projects. We expanded our catalog while keeping a strict standard for quality control.

Today, our team works on projects across residential, commercial, and hospitality spaces, providing solutions that balance performance with design.
