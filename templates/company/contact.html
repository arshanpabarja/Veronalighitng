Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?

+

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A simple retrofit can take 2–4 weeks, while a comprehensive facade lighting system may take 8–12 weeks from design to installation.

Do you provide design consultation?

+

Yes. We offer free initial consultation to discuss your vision, requirements, and budget. Our lighting design team will create a proposal tailored to your project.

What warranty do you offer?

+

All Verona luminaires come with a 5-year warranty on components and labor. Extended warranties and service plans are available upon request.

Can you retrofit existing spaces?

+

Absolutely. We specialize in upgrading existing lighting systems. Our team will assess your current setup and recommend the best solutions that minimize disruption.

How do I request a quote?

+

You can reach out via this contact form, email our sales team, or call us directly. Share your project details and we'll prepare a comprehensive quote.
